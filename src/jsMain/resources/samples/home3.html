For 20 years, we’ve built custom solutions across diverse industries, helping create innovative products and experiences. Our commitment to quality and performance has led to trusted partnerships that stand the test of time.

Key Industries

Automotive

We develop ADAS features (collision avoidance, lane keeping, ASIL-D safety), handle in-vehicle networking (CAN, LIN, FlexRay), and implement powertrain control & battery management. Our ISO 26262, AUTOSAR, and ASPICE compliance ensures safe, modern systems—from secure diagnostics to seamless OTA updates.

MedTech & Industry 4.0

From predictive maintenance in smart factories to secure communication in healthcare, we embed compliance-driven software and robust connectivity (BLE, Wi-Fi, industrial protocols) to optimize performance and ensure safety-critical reliability. Cryptography, authentication, and remote data management are built into every solution.

Platforms & Applications

We leverage Flutter, Kotlin, React, and more to craft stable apps—be it for smartphones, automotive infotainment, or cloud-based dashboards. Our CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab) enable daily development with integration, staging, and automated testing. With asynchronous programming and performance tuning at the forefront, we deliver offline capabilities, secure role-based access, and real-time analytics.
