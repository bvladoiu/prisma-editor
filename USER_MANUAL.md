# DRAFT - Prisma Editor - Your Friendly Guide

Welcome to the Prisma Editor! This guide will help you create and manage awesome website content with ease.

## Let's Get Started

Just open the Prisma Editor in your web browser using the URL you've been given. No complicated setup needed!

### What You'll See

Here's what you'll find in the editor:

- **Top Bar**: Where you'll find navigation controls and info about which site you're on
- **Main Area**: The big space in the middle where you'll see and edit your page
- **Round Button**: That handy circle at the bottom right that lets you start editing
- **Bottom Panel**: Pops up when you're editing to let you pick your site and language
- **Side Menu**: Slides in from the side when you need to jump to different pages

## Making Changes is Easy!

### Picking Your Site and Language

1. Click the pencil icon (✏️) in the round button at the bottom right
2. A drawer will pop up from the bottom with dropdown menus
3. Pick which website you want to work on (like ContaDeal or PRISMA-Software)
4. Choose your language (the options will change based on the site you picked)
5. Hit the save icon (💾) when you're ready

### Changing Content

1. Click that same pencil icon (✏️) to start editing
2. Now you can click on any text to change it
3. You'll also see some helpful buttons appear:
   - Minus signs (-) let you delete things you don't want
   - Plus signs (+) let you add new content
4. Make all the changes you want
5. When you're happy with everything, click the save icon (💾)

### Building Blocks You Can Use

Here are the different pieces you can play with:

- **Text**: Words and paragraphs in different styles
- **Images**: Pictures that can have captions
- **Sections**: Containers that organize your content
- **Article Cards**: Neat boxes showing article info (title, who wrote it, when)
- **Hero**: The eye-catching stuff at the top of your page
- **Expertise**: Special sections to highlight knowledge areas

## Playing with the Building Blocks

### Working with Text

- Just click on any text and start typing
- Don't worry about formatting - the right styles (big headlines, normal text, etc.) happen automatically

### Working with Images

- Change where an image comes from by editing its URL
- Add fun captions to explain what people are seeing
- Make sure to add alt text so everyone can enjoy your content

### Working with Sections

- Sections help organize your page into neat chunks
- Change the title to something catchy
- Use the + button to add more stuff inside
- Use the - button to remove things you don't need

### Working with Article Cards

- Update titles, authors, and dates with just a few clicks
- These usually live in the "Latest" section of your page

## Your Playground: The Catalog Page

Think of the Catalog page as your sandbox where you can:

1. See all the cool building blocks available to you
2. Practice editing without breaking anything important
3. Learn how different pieces work together

## Quick Tips for Success

- Double-check your site and language before you start editing
- Hit that save button often - better safe than sorry!
- Try to keep your pages looking consistent
- Take a moment to preview your work before you call it done
- Have fun with it - that's what makes great content!

## Oops! Fixing Common Problems

### When Things Go Wrong

- **Your changes disappeared**: Did you hit the save button? That little 💾 icon needs a click!
- **Can't see language options**: You need to pick a site first, then the language options will appear
- **Can't edit anything**: Make sure you clicked the pencil icon (✏️) to turn on edit mode

### Need More Help?

Stuck on something not covered here? No worries! Reach out to your friendly support team and they'll get you back on track in no time.

## Quick Reference: What's What

- **The Round Button (FAB)**: That handy circle at the bottom right of your screen
- **Building Block**: Any piece you can add to your page (text, image, section, etc.)
- **Site**: Which website you're working on (like ContaDeal or PRISMA-Software)
- **TAG**: Just a behind-the-scenes label that helps identify different parts
- **Bottom Drawer**: The panel that slides up from the bottom when you're editing
